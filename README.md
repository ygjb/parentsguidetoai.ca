# A Parent's Guide to Artificial Intelligence

A plain-language resource to help parents and caregivers understand AI tools their children encounter at school and at home — what they're good for, where the risks are, and exactly who to call if something goes wrong. No technical background required.

**Version 1.2 · Reviewed June 2026 · B.C. Schools & at Home**

---

## What this is

This guide was written in response to the Vancouver School Board's rollout of **Microsoft Copilot 13+** for eligible secondary students, and the broader reality that AI is now embedded in the tools children use every day — at school and at home.

The guide covers:

- **What AI actually is** — a plain-language explanation without jargon
- **VSB's Copilot 13+** — what protections it includes and what questions to ask
- **Opportunities** — how AI can support learning when used well
- **Risks and challenges** — privacy, misinformation, deepfakes, sextortion, emotional dependency, and more
- **The AI traffic light** — a quick framework for what's generally okay, what needs care, and what to avoid
- **A family AI agreement** — conversation-ready expectations for home use
- **Conversation starters** — questions to open a dialogue with your child
- **If something goes wrong** — specific B.C. and Canadian contacts and resources, including Take Back Your Images, NeedHelpNow.ca, Cybertip.ca, erase — Report It, and Kids Help Phone
- **FAQ** — common parent questions answered directly
- **A plain-language glossary** — definitions of terms used throughout

## Running locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser — no build step, server, or dependencies to install. Bootstrap 5 and Bootstrap Icons load from cdnjs.cloudflare.com with Subresource Integrity (SRI) verification. The Figtree heading font falls back to the system font stack if not installed locally; Google Fonts is not used.
